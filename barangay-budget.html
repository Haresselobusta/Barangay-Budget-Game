    Barangay Budget

    You are the Punong Barangay for the next 120 days. Floods, typhoons, fires and earthquakes will test your town. Your treasury is limited, so decide where the money goes.

    • Every department loses funds each day. Keep the bars from running dry.
    • Healthcare cuts deaths during disasters. Fire crews put out fires sooner.
    • Law Enforcement raises tax income. Education grows the population.
    • Roads decide how fast every other project is delivered.
